Conflict resolution and one-way syncs.

- Resolve conflicts without leaving the app: choose a starting conflict
  policy per profile, then decide local, remote, or keep-both for each
  conflicting file.
- Long-press a profile to run one-way operations: copy to or from the
  server (never deletes the other side) or mirror to or from it (an
  exact copy, with a confirmation), plus a test-connection action.
- Sync results are more accurate: runs that only skipped items are no
  longer reported as failures, and files kept by a policy are no longer
  listed as conflicts.
