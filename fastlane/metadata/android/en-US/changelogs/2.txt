Restore Unison's archive locking.

- The bundled Unison now uses an O_EXCL archive lock instead of disabling
  locking (previously forced by Android's SELinux policy). If another client is
  already syncing the same server roots, UnisonDroid now stops instead of
  risking archive corruption.
- Stale lock files left by an interrupted sync are cleared automatically.
- Fix a flaky SSH tunnel teardown test.
